Get Started

When a template is not the shape of your business.

A template decides the pages, the order and the shape before it knows anything about you, and most of the time that is fine. When it is not — when the way you sell does not fit a layout somebody else drew — we start from what you actually do and build the site around it.

Start a custom build

What you get

Three levels, depending on how much already exists.

Site only

You have a name, a logo and a rough idea of what the pages should say. We design and build the site, put your words and pictures in, connect your domain and launch it.

Site and brand

There is no logo yet, or it was made in a hurry and you have never liked it. We settle the name treatment, the colours and the type first, then build on top of that.

Site, brand and the tools around it

The site, plus the things that actually bring work in: enquiry forms, booking, email, review links and analytics, set up and working on the day it goes live.

What we build

Different businesses, same standard.

  • Storefronts

    Shopify, built around your products instead of bending a theme until it nearly fits.

  • Service businesses

    Trades, clinics, studios. Prices visible, and a way to book that works on a phone held in one hand.

  • Food and venues

    A menu you can change yourself, opening hours that are right, and a table booked in two taps.

  • Portfolios and galleries

    The work first and large, with a contact path that does not get lost underneath it.

  • Launches

    One page, one date, one thing for the visitor to do.

Process

You see it in the browser halfway through, not at the end.

  1. 01

    A call, then a brief

    What the business does, who buys from you, and what has to happen on the site for it to be worth building. Written down, so we are both working from the same page.

  2. 02

    Structure and words

    Which pages exist, in what order, and what each one says. Agreed before anything is designed, because moving a sentence is cheap and moving a page is not.

  3. 03

    Design in the browser

    A real link you can open on your own phone, not a flat picture of a website. You tell us what to change while it is still easy to change.

  4. 04

    Build and test

    Real devices and real browsers. Every link followed, every form submitted, every page checked at phone, tablet and desktop width.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Domain connected, the site live, and your email left exactly as it was.

Before we start

What we will need from you.

  • Your logo

    As a file rather than a screenshot of one. If you do not have it, say so and we will make one.

  • Pictures you are allowed to use

    Your own photos are best. We will tell you what is missing and what we can source.

  • What you sell, and for how much

    Services or products, with the prices you want shown. If prices are not public, that is a decision, not a blocker.

  • Whatever words already exist

    An old site, a flyer, a rough list in your notes. Anything beats a blank page.

  • Access to your domain

    The login for wherever it is registered, or the go-ahead for us to buy one for you.

  • One person who decides

    Two people with different opinions is the single most common reason a build runs long.

FAQ

Get Started

How does AmazeWeb work?

Choose a template, purchase it through our checkout, and receive everything you need to customize and launch your website.

What platforms do you support?

AmazeWeb focuses on Shopify templates and custom-built sites, with additional services for website setup, email design, and brand assets.

Can you set up the website for me?

Yes. If you want help, you can add a setup service and we’ll install the template, connect basic pages, and prepare the site for launch.

Design

Can I customize the design?

Yes. You can update colors, fonts, images, sections, buttons, and copy so the template matches your brand.

Are the templates mobile-friendly?

Yes. AmazeWeb templates are designed to look clean on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes. If you need something more specific than a template, you can book a custom website or brand design service.

Setup

Can AmazeWeb set up the website for me?

Yes. You can add setup support if you want us to install the template and prepare the website for launch.

What do I need before setup starts?

You’ll need your logo, brand colors, images, website copy, product info, and access to your Shopify account or hosting.

Can you connect my domain?

Yes. Domain connection can be included with setup support, depending on your platform and hosting provider.

Support

What happens after I purchase?

After purchase, you’ll receive access to the template files, setup instructions, and any included resources.

Can I get help after purchase?

Yes. Basic support is available for questions about downloading, accessing, or using your template.

Do you provide long-term website management?

AmazeWeb focuses on templates, setup, and launch support. Ongoing maintenance may be offered separately depending on availability.
