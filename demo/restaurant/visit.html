Finding us

Down the lane off the main road, past the bike shop. There is no sign above the door, only the number, which we have been told is annoying and have decided to keep.

Last orders half an hour before close, and the kitchen genuinely stops then — it is one fire and it has to come down.

Getting here

TrainEight minutes from the station, turn left out of the main entrance
BusThe 4, 12 and 41 stop at the end of the lane
CarNo parking on the lane. The multi-storey on Cross Street is two minutes and free after six.
BikeRacks outside the bike shop next door, covered
AccessTwo steps at the door with a ramp on request. One level inside, 90cm doorway, accessible toilet.

What the room is like

Thirty-four seats, one long room, hard floor and hard walls, so it is loud when it is full. That is a choice and it is not for everyone — if you need to hear across the table, lunch is much quieter than Friday dinner.

Four seats at the pass look straight into the fire. They are walk-in only, they are the best seats in the building, and we will not hold them for anyone.

We do not play music during service. The fire is quite loud enough.

There is no dress code, no minimum spend, and no separate bar — if you want a glass of wine and the bread you are welcome to take a table for that, and nobody will do the thing with the eyebrows.

Questions we get

VegetariansProperly catered for at every service, not as an afterthought. Vegans, tell us a day ahead and it will be good.
AllergiesTell us when you book and again when you sit. The kitchen is small, so we will be honest about cross-contact.
ChildrenWelcome at every service. High chairs and smaller portions.
DogsYes, in the front half, water by the door.
Service chargeNone added. Prices are what you pay, and the team is paid properly whether or not you tip.
Gift vouchersAny amount, no expiry. Ask at the pass or email us.
