How we do this

HoldingTables are yours for two hours at dinner, ninety minutes at lunch. Nobody will be moved on if the table behind you is not booked.
LateFifteen minutes and we will still have the table. Longer than that, call us and we will almost always make it work.
Six or morePhone, not the form. Large tables change how the whole room runs and we would rather talk it through.
DepositsOnly on Sundays, 10 a seat, taken off the bill. A single sitting means an empty chair cannot be resold.
CancellingAny time up to the day before, no charge, no guilt. Text the number on your confirmation.
Walk-insA third of the room, every service, including the four seats at the pass which are the best in the house.
ChildrenWelcome at every service. High chairs, smaller portions, and nobody minds the noise.
DogsYes, in the front half. There is water by the door.
AccessOne level, 90cm door, accessible toilet. Two steps outside and a ramp we are happy to put down.
