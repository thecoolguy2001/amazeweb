About us

Nocturne is two people, a heat press and a projector, in a unit off Memorial Drive in Atlanta.

One film a season

We pick a film in the first week of the season and watch it until somebody writes something down. Whatever gets written down ends up on a shirt. The season is named after the film, the pieces are named after what happens in it, and the clip running behind this shop is the one we are on now.

Nine pieces, then we stop

A season is nine pieces and no more. Runs are fifty per colourway, numbered inside the hem, and nothing gets reprinted once it has sold through — not because of scarcity as a tactic, but because the next film is already picked.

Printed here

Every piece is pulled by hand, one colour at a time, in the same room the orders are packed in. The registration is not perfect and neither is the coverage. That is what hand-printing looks like up close, and we would rather you knew that before it arrives than after.

What we will not do

No seasonal sales, no bundling, no discount code that appears when you move your mouse towards the back button. The ten percent on the tab at the side of the page is the only one there is, it is for a first order, and it is not coming back around in November.
