How each one actually fits

The Overshirt

Generous. It is meant to go over a crew, so if you only ever wear it alone, size down. The sleeve is deliberately a little short.

Lambswool Crew

True to size with a close body and a high neck. It relaxes about a centimetre in the chest after two or three wears and stays there.

Wide Trouser

Sits on the waist, not the hip, and is wide from there down. If you are between waists, take the smaller — the cloth gives.

Heavy Tee

Boxy and heavy at 240gsm. It does not drape, and that is deliberate. Size up only if you want it genuinely oversized.

Chore Jacket

The roomiest thing we make. Most people take their normal size and wear a knit under it quite happily.

Quilted Liner

Cut to go under the chore jacket, so it is slimmer than everything else. Take your usual size and expect it to be close.

Measuring
yourself

Chest. Lay a shirt you like flat, button it, smooth it out, and measure straight across from armpit to armpit. Double it. That is the number in the table.

Waist. Same again with trousers — flat, across the top of the waistband, doubled.

Still stuck. Email us what you own and what size it is and we will tell you which of ours matches. We would rather spend five minutes on that than process a return.
