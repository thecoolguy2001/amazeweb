Four cloths,
five colours,
one dyehouse

Everything in the capsule is cut from four cloths: a 420gsm cotton twill, a lambswool, a wool-linen suiting, and a heavyweight jersey. All four go to the same dyehouse in Leicester and come back in the same five colours.

It means the sage overshirt and the sage trousers are genuinely the same sage, which sounds obvious and almost never is. It also means we restock rather than replace — the crew you bought in 2024 is still the crew, in the same colour, if you wear a hole in it.
