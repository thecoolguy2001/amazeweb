Fabric and care
100% cotton twill, 420gsm, garment dyed in Leicester. Machine wash cold, line dry. It will fade half a shade in the first few washes and then stop, which is the point of garment dyeing.
Where it is made
Cut and sewn in Leicester, eleven miles from the dyehouse. We visit about once a month and the factory is audited annually by an independent body whose report we will send you if you ask.
Delivery and returns
Free UK delivery over £120, otherwise £4.95, next day if ordered before 2pm. Ninety days to return anything unworn, free, sale included.
