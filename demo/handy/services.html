Plumbing

Drains, leaks, fixtures, shut-offs, repipes. If water is going somewhere it should not, this is the right call.

Vans carry the common parts, so roughly four jobs in five are finished on the first visit. When we have to order something, you get the part number and the price before we leave.

Larger work we quote in writing

Repiping, bathroom rough-ins, sewer line replacement, and anything that means opening a wall. You get a fixed written quote after a free survey, valid for 60 days.

Heating and hot water

Water heaters, boilers, radiators, thermostats, and the annual service that stops most of it going wrong in the first place.

No heat is treated as an emergency between November and March regardless of what the booking form says. We will reshuffle the day for it.

Annual service, $119

Full check, flue test, written report, and a reminder next year. Twelve months of priority booking comes with it.

Electrical

Faults, panels, outlets, lighting, EV chargers, and safety inspections for anyone buying or selling.

Every circuit we touch gets tested and recorded. You get the readings, not just a verbal all-clear, which matters when you sell the house.

Safety inspection, $235

Full property check against current code, with a photographed report and a priced list of anything that needs attention.
