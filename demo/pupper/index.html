A day here

Hour by hour, so you know

This is the actual shape of a daycare day. Dogs do better when it is the same one every time.

7:00

Doors open

Drop off any time to half nine. We take the lead at the gate rather than in the yard so arrivals stay calm.

8:30

First play

Two groups, split by size and how boisterous they are rather than by breed. Twenty minutes of settling before anything gets going.

10:30

Out on the common

Small groups of six with two of us, forty minutes, on lead to the gate and off once we are through it. Not in heavy rain or above 24°C.

12:00

Lunch, then down

Food if you have sent it. Then two hours of actual rest in the quiet room with the lights down. This is the part that makes the evening bearable for you.

14:00

Enrichment

Scent games, puzzle feeders, and the paddling pool from May. Tired noses beat tired legs.

15:30

Second play

Shorter and calmer. Any grooming or training slots happen around now, one dog at a time.

16:30

Wind down

Chews, brushing, and the day sheet gets written — what they ate, who they played with, anything we noticed.

17:00

Collection

Any time to seven. You get the day sheet and, if something was off, a conversation rather than a note.
