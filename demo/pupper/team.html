Who they are

A bit more

Sam

Started this in a back garden in 2017

Was a vet nurse for six years before that, which is why the first-aid training here is not a tick-box exercise. Boards up to four dogs at home. Owns Dot, a thirteen-year-old terrier who supervises from a chair.

Nadia

Runs the floor and the groups

Decides who plays with whom, which is the single most important job here. Did the canine behaviour certificate in her own time and keeps a notebook on every dog. Owns two greyhounds who have never once hurried.

Joel

Eleven years grooming, two of them in a rescue

Which is where he learned to work with dogs who have been badly handled before. Will refuse to finish a groom if a dog is distressed and will tell you why. Owns a very large, very anxious poodle called Mop.

Priya

IMDT accredited, reward based, no exceptions

Takes the one-to-ones and will tell you straight when a dog needs a clinical behaviourist rather than a trainer. Has a collie who can do more than most people and is still, in her words, a nightmare.
