Daycare

7:00 to 19:00. Two play groups split by size and temperament, a walk on the common mid-morning, and two hours of genuine rest after lunch.

Half days to 13:00 are 60% of the day rate. Late collection after 19:00 is £10 and we would much rather you just called.

£30 – £40per day, by size

Grooming

Full groom, bath and tidy, or nails only. Separate quiet room, hand dried, never a cage dryer, and we stop if a dog is distressed rather than push through.

Matting that needs clipping out is quoted before we start, never after. Nails on their own are £12 and take five minutes.

£42 – £57full groom, by size

Training

One to one, reward based, hour long. In the yard, on the common, or at your house if the problem only happens there.

Blocks of six are 10% off. We do not do group classes and we will say so if your dog needs a behaviourist instead of us.

£35 – £47per session, by size

Boarding

Overnight in Sam's house, maximum four dogs, no kennel block. Walked twice, fed on your schedule, and a photo at bedtime.

Only for dogs who already come to daycare, because we need to know them first. Christmas books out by September.

£40 – £54per night, by size

The small print

Things worth knowing before you book

What do you need from us before the first day?

Vaccination record including kennel cough, a flea and worm treatment in the last three months, and your vet's details. Dogs need to be neutered from twelve months for the group — not a judgement, just what keeps the group stable. We will take all of it by photo on the day.

What happens if my dog does not settle?

We tell you the same day, at pickup, face to face. Some dogs are not group dogs and that is a fact about the dog, not a failure. If that is the answer after the trial we will say so and refund the half day, and usually suggest one-to-one walks instead.

Do you ever use crates or corrections?

Crates only at nap time, doors open, for dogs who choose to use them — several do. No slip leads, no prong collars, no spray bottles, no shouting. If a dog needs separating it goes to the quiet room with a person, not on its own.

What is the waiting list like?

Honest answer: Tuesdays and Thursdays are usually three to four months. Mondays and Fridays are often available within a fortnight. We cap at twenty-four dogs a day and we will not go over it to clear the list faster.

What if my dog gets hurt?

Minor scrapes get cleaned and written on the day sheet with a call if it is anything more than that. Anything worse and we go straight to the vet on Common Road — we are five minutes away — and phone you from the car. We carry £5m public liability and every member of staff holds a canine first-aid certificate.

Can we visit before booking?

Yes, and you should. Thursday afternoons at two, no appointment needed. You will see the actual yard with the actual dogs in it rather than a tidied version.
