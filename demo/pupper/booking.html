Book a trial day

One day, half price, no commitment either way. We find out whether your dog is happy in a group; you find out whether we are any good.

1

You send this form

Takes two minutes. We reply within a day, usually the same morning.

2

Ten minutes on the phone

About your dog, not about selling you anything. Recall, other dogs, anything that has gone wrong before.

3

Trial day

Drop off by nine. We watch how the introduction goes and keep it short if it is not working.

4

We tell you the truth

At pickup. Including if the answer is that this is the wrong setting for your dog.

We reply within one working day. Your details are used to arrange the booking and nothing else — no newsletter, no card stored.

Bring with you

On the first day

Vaccination record

Including kennel cough, done at least two weeks before. A photo on your phone is fine.

Flea and worm

Treated within the last three months. Tell us the product and we will note the date.

Your vet's details

Practice name and number, and permission to take your dog there if we have to.

Their own food

If they eat at lunchtime. Labelled, in one bag, with the amount written on it.

Leave the lead and collar on at drop-off; we take them off once the group has settled and they go back on before you arrive. Do not bring toys from home — they cause more arguments than they are worth.
