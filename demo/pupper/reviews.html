About the three-star ones

What we did about them

Mud

Fair, and partly fixed

We resurfaced two thirds of the yard in 2025 with a proper drainage layer. The far end is still grass and still turns to soup in February. Towels by the gate; we are saving for the rest.

Waiting list

Fair, and not going to change

Twenty-four dogs a day is the cap and we will not lift it. Mondays and Fridays usually have space within a fortnight if you can be flexible.

Boarding rule

Fair, and staying

We only board dogs we already know from daycare. A strange dog in someone's house overnight is how things go wrong, and we would rather lose the booking.

Invoicing

Fair, and being fixed

Email invoices go out monthly and they have been late twice. We are moving to a proper system in the new year, which is boring but it is the most common complaint we get.

Read the bad ones, then come and look anyway.

Thursdays at two. First day is half price and you owe nothing if you walk away after it.
