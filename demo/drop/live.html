Questions people actually ask.

When does it ship?

Orders are fulfilled in the order they are placed, starting 5 March. The first batch is 400 units. If you are outside that batch you will be told before you pay, not after.

Is there a cheaper version coming?

No. There is one camera at one price. The graphite, bone and yellow finishes cost the same.

Can I get it serviced?

Yes. Parts lists and service drawings are published, and any competent camera technician can work on it. We also service it ourselves for a flat fee after the warranty ends.

What about returns?

Thirty days, shot or unshot, provided the body is not damaged. Return shipping is on us within the EU, UK and US.

Do you ship outside those regions?

Yes, to 41 countries. Duties are calculated at checkout so the price you see is the price you pay.
