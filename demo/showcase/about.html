About

One designer, long enough to know which corners not to cut.

I design identities and interfaces, usually for organisations at the point where the thing they built by hand has stopped scaling.

Fifteen years, the first nine inside agencies in London and Copenhagen, independent since 2019. The agency years taught me how to run a process. Going independent taught me how much of that process was for the agency's benefit rather than the client's.

I work on two projects at a time, directly, with no account layer. You talk to the person doing the work, which means fewer meetings and faster decisions, and it means I say no to things that do not fit.

Teaching at the Lisbon school of design one term a year. Occasionally writing about why design systems rot. Always available for a twenty-minute call before anyone commits to anything.
