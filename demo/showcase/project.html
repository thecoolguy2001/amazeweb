2025 · Product interface and design system

Meridian Health

A clinical records platform used by 1,400 staff across eleven sites, rebuilt around the four screens people actually live in.

Client
Meridian Health Group
Year
2025, nine months
Role
Design lead, sole designer
Shipped
240 components, 31 screens

The brief

Meridian had eleven sites running three different versions of the same software, and a nursing team who had quietly built their own paper workarounds for all of it.

The ask was a redesign. The actual problem was that nobody had watched anyone use the thing in four years. We started with two weeks on ward, not in Figma, and the paper workarounds turned out to be the real specification.

Four screens accounted for 86% of session time. Everything else was a long tail that could wait. That single number reorganised the whole project.

What we built

A component library with 240 pieces, each one documented with the clinical situation it exists for. A shift handover view that replaced the paper. And a records search that returns in under 400ms on the ten-year-old terminals the trusts refuse to replace, because those terminals are not going anywhere.

The system ships as code, not as a Figma file pretending to be code. Meridian's two front-end engineers extended it twice in the first quarter without asking me a question, which is the only review I wanted.

Outcome

Handover time fell from an average of 22 minutes to 9. Support tickets tagged "can't find" dropped by just over half. The paper workarounds are gone, which the ward sister described as the first time software had taken something off her desk instead of adding to it.
