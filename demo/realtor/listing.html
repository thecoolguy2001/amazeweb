Mill Quarter · built 1912 · freehold

18 Calder Street

$624,000$359 per sq ft · 3 days listed

A brick terrace with a good roof and a bad kitchen.

Built in 1912, three bedrooms over two floors, south-facing at the back. The roof was replaced in 2019 and the certificate is in the pack. The boiler is from 2016 and was serviced in March.

The kitchen is original to a 1990s refit and is the main reason this is priced at $359 a square foot rather than the $385 the two recent sales on this street would suggest. Budget somewhere around $28,000 to put that right; we can send you the two quotes the sellers already have.

There is no off-street parking and there will not be — the terrace has no rear access. The permit scheme on Calder has a waiting list that has been about four months for the last two years.

It is a ten-minute walk to the Mill Quarter station and twenty-two minutes from there to the centre. The primary school two streets over is in band B and has places; the catchment line runs along Lower Calder, so check before you assume.
