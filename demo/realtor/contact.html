What a valuation involves

About forty minutes, and you get a document at the end of it.

1 · We walk round

Forty minutes, room by room, measuring. We will ask about the boiler, the roof, and anything that has been done without permission — not to catch you out, but because a buyer's surveyor will find it anyway and it is cheaper to know now.

2 · You get it in writing

Within two working days: three genuinely comparable sales, what we would list at, what we think it closes at, and the two or three things worth fixing first. If the honest answer is that now is a bad time to sell, that is what it will say.

Our fee is 2.1% plus tax, the same for every property, with no tiering and no sliding scale. It covers photography, floor plans, the portals, viewings conducted by us, and negotiation. There is no charge if it does not sell.
