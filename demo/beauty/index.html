What is in it

The four that do the work

Everything else in the bottle is there to carry these, keep them stable, or stop them stinging.

Ascorbic acid

10% · Bright Serum

The best-evidenced brightening ingredient there is, and the most temperamental. Ours is at pH 3.2 in an opaque bottle and it will still go amber in about six months, which is normal and not a fault.

Retinaldehyde

0.3% · Renew Serum

A step closer to retinoic acid than retinol, so it works at lower strength with less irritation. Start twice a week. Anyone promising no adjustment period is selling you something weak.

Niacinamide

4% · Calm Serum

Four per cent, not ten. Above about five the evidence stops improving and the chance of flushing goes up, so we stopped where the research did.
