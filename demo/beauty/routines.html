The rule

Thinnest to thickest, and SPF last

1 — Cleanse

Morning and evening

Once at night is enough unless you wear heavy SPF or makeup, in which case the milk first and the gel second. In the morning, plenty of people are fine with water alone and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

2 — Treat

One active at a time

On damp skin, thinnest first. Never two new actives in the same week — if something stings you need to know which thing it was.

3 — Seal and protect

Every morning, no exceptions

Moisturiser, then SPF over the top in the morning. SPF is the last step always; putting anything over it dilutes the film and the number on the bottle stops being true.
