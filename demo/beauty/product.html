Full ingredients, with percentages
Aqua, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Glycerin (8%), Dicaprylyl Carbonate, Retinaldehyde (0.3%), Niacinamide (2%), Squalane, Tocopherol (0.5%), Bisabolol (0.3%), Hydroxyethylcellulose, Sodium Hyaluronate (0.2%), Pentylene Glycol, Sodium Phytate, Citric Acid, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin.

That is the whole list and those are the real numbers. Anything under 1% is still listed rather than hidden behind “and other ingredients”.

How to start
Twice a week for the first fortnight, at night, on dry skin, after cleansing and before moisturiser. Build to three times a week in weeks three and four. Most people settle at every other night and never need to go further. Expect some flaking around the nose and chin in the first month — that is the adjustment, not a reaction.
What not to use with it
Not on the same night as the Bright Serum, and not alongside any other exfoliant while you are building up. If you are using prescription tretinoin, you do not need this. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, do not use it at all, and your GP will say the same.
Shelf life and storage
Twelve months unopened, six once opened. Keep the cap on and keep it out of the bathroom window — retinaldehyde is light sensitive, which is why the bottle is opaque and why we will not sell it in a clear one.
Delivery and returns
Free over £50, otherwise £3.95. Two samples with every order. Sixty days to return it even if it is opened and half used — if your skin disagreed with it we would rather know than have you keep it.
