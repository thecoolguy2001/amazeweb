Before you ask

The four most common questions

Is the taster really free, and really no pressure?

Yes to both. One session at any club, no cost, and nobody phones you afterwards. If your child wants to come back you will tell us; if they do not, that is genuinely useful information and we would rather have it than a reluctant booking.

What if we can only do some weeks?

Weekly clubs are termly because the group matters and children do better with the same faces. Holiday camps and after-school are booked by the day, so if your pattern is irregular those are the flexible ones.

My child does not know anyone. Will they be all right?

Most of them arrive not knowing anyone. New children get paired with someone on their first two sessions, and a member of staff stays close without hovering. If they are miserable at the end of a taster we will say so rather than sell you a term.

Can I stay and watch?

For the under-3 sessions you stay anyway. For everything else you are welcome to stay for the first session and most parents find it easier to go for a coffee and come back — children settle faster when you are not in the room, which is annoying but consistently true.
