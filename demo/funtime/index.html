For parents

The bits that actually matter

Everyone is checked

Enhanced DBS for every member of staff, renewed every three years, and paediatric first aid for all of them rather than one designated person. The certificates are on the wall by the door.

You can cancel

Weekly sessions are paid by the term but cancelled any time with two weeks' notice and we refund the rest. No joining fee and no contract longer than a term.

Nobody is turned away

We accept funded hours and tax-free childcare, and we hold six subsidised places a term. If cost is the problem, email us — the conversation is private and it is not awkward.
