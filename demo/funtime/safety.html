Staffing

Who is in the room

Enhanced DBS, everyone

Every member of staff and every volunteer, renewed on a three-year cycle, checked against the barred list. Numbers are on the register and we will show it to you.

First aid, everyone

Full 12-hour paediatric first aid for all staff, not one named person. Refreshed every three years. Two defibrillator-trained staff on site at all times.

Two adults, always

No child is ever alone with one adult, including on the minibus and including at the toilet door. Nobody here works a session on their own.

Named safeguarding lead

Nadia Croft, level 3 trained, with a deputy who is on site when she is not. Both numbers are on the noticeboard and the policy is on the wall, not in a drawer.

Signed in, signed out

Electronic register. You get a text when your child arrives and another when they leave, with who collected them. Only people on your named list can take them.

Nut free, properly

Nothing with nuts comes into the building, including in packed snacks. Four children here carry adrenaline pens and all staff know which four and where the pens are.

Policies

Written down, and short

Behaviour

We do not use a naughty step, a traffic-light chart, or withdrawal of food. A child who is struggling gets a person, a quiet corner, and a conversation. If something keeps happening we talk to you about it early rather than presenting it as a problem three months in. Physical intervention is only ever to stop immediate harm and is always logged and always told to you the same day.

Accidents and illness

Bumps and scrapes get cleaned and written in the accident book, which you sign at collection. Anything to the head means we phone you straight away whether or not it looks serious. Children with a temperature, sickness or diarrhoea cannot be in the building and need 48 hours clear — hard on parents, and the only thing that stops a club closing for a week.

Photographs

Opt in, and it is a real choice with no follow-up question. No photographs on social media of any child without written consent from a parent, no names ever attached, and staff phones stay in lockers during sessions. The camera is the centre's and the card never leaves the building.

Collection

Only adults on your named list, and we will ask for ID the first time even if it is obviously you. If someone turns up who is not on the list we phone you and they wait outside, which is awkward exactly once. Late collection after 18:30 is £8 per fifteen minutes and we would much rather you called than rushed.

Complaints

Tell any member of staff and it goes to Nadia the same day. Written response within five working days. If that is not good enough you can go straight to Ofsted and the number is on the noticeboard — we put it there ourselves.

Additional needs

What we can and cannot do

Two of our staff hold the level 3 SEND qualification and we currently support eleven children with an EHCP or an identified need. Quiet space, ear defenders, visual timetables and a predictable session shape are all normal here rather than special arrangements.

What we cannot do is one-to-one support out of our own staffing. If your child needs a dedicated adult, that has to come through the local authority or privately, and we will help you make that case — we have done it successfully four times.

What we will never do is tell you a place is unavailable when the real reason is that your child is harder work. If we genuinely cannot meet a need safely we will say exactly that, in person, and tell you who in the area can.
