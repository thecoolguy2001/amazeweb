Fabric and care
100% double-faced wool, 680gsm, woven at a mill in Biella. Dry clean only, and not often — wool wants airing far more than it wants solvent. Hang it somewhere with a draught for a day and most things lift out on their own.
Who made it
Cut and sewn at a twelve-person workshop in Porto we have used since the first collection. A run of forty takes them about a week. They are paid per garment at a rate we agreed together and have raised twice.
Shipping and returns
Free shipping over £150, otherwise £5.95. Sent in 2–3 working days from Porto. Thirty days to return anything unworn with the tag on, free, in the bag it came in. Repairs are free for as long as the coat lasts.
