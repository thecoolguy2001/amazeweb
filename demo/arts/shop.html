How this works

Everything is printed in Lisbon by Oficina Vinte, signed and numbered in the studio, and checked before it goes out.

Shipping is flat: €18 within Europe, €38 elsewhere, in a rigid tube. Framing is not offered — a local framer will do better work than anything that survives a courier.

If an edition sells out it stays listed, marked sold, so the numbers stay honest.
