Paintings about weather, water, and the hour before it turns.

Oil and distemper on linen, made slowly in a former tile works outside Setúbal. Twelve to fourteen paintings a year.

Represented by Galeria Brun in Lisbon and Mahler & Sohn in Vienna. Work is held in four public collections. Studio visits by appointment on the first Thursday of the month.

One painting, at length

The pumping station runs at night because electricity is cheaper then. I painted it over nine months, mostly from memory, and the colour kept getting darker than the place ever was. Acquired by the Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation in March 2026 for the permanent collection. Shown first at Galeria Brun, then in Water Rights at the Pavilhão Branco.
