Questions

The things people ask before they put a card in.

What counts as an engineer?

Anyone who can be paged. People who only read the status page or the postmortems are free and unlimited — a product manager watching an incident should not cost you fourteen dollars.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Paging keeps working for seven days past the end of the trial, because switching an alerting tool off without warning is a genuinely dangerous default. After that the account drops to Solo rather than locking.

Do you charge for alerts, checks, or SMS?

No. Unlimited on every paid plan, including voice calls. If the price of a page went up with the number of pages, we would have a reason to keep you noisy.

Is there a discount for non-profits or open source?

Team is free for registered non-profits and for maintainers of open-source projects with public infrastructure. One email, no form.

Where does our data live?

Frankfurt by default, with Virginia and Sydney available on Company. Incident data is kept for two years, then deleted. Exports are available at any time and are not a retention plan.
