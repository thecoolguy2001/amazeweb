The product

Four steps, and only the second one needs you awake.

Nettle sits between the things that tell you something is wrong and the people who can fix it. Here is the whole loop.

1 · Detect

Point Nettle at what you already have — Prometheus, CloudWatch, a health endpoint, a webhook from anything. A check has to fail for a full interval before it counts, so a blip stays a blip.

Checks run every 30s from three regions

2 · Page

One person, chosen from service ownership, reached by SMS and phone call. No answer in four minutes and it moves to the next owner, then to everyone.

Reply to the SMS to acknowledge — no app needed

3 · Record

From the moment the incident opens, every alert, deploy, rollback, and comment is written to one ordered timeline with real timestamps.

Nothing to remember to turn on

4 · Close

When the signal has been healthy for five minutes Nettle resolves the incident and drafts the postmortem, with the timeline filled and the judgement calls left blank.

Export to Markdown, Notion, or Linear

Quiet by default

Alerts that have fired more than three times in a week without a human acting get flagged as noise, with a one-click mute and a note in the weekly digest.

Teams cut 31% of pages in the first month

Honest status page

A public page that updates itself from real incidents, not from someone remembering to post. Your uptime figure is computed, not typed.

Custom domain on every paid plan
