What it does

Three jobs, done properly, instead of thirty done badly.

Most incident tools are built for companies with a rota, a runbook, and someone whose job this is. This one is built for the eight-person team where that person is also shipping features.

Wakes one person

No rota to maintain. Nettle reads your repository ownership and pages whoever last touched the service that is failing — then escalates on a timer if they do not answer.

Median time to acknowledge: 94 seconds

Writes the timeline

Every alert, deploy, rollback, and message lands in one ordered record as it happens. Nobody has to reconstruct the night from six Slack threads and a terminal history.

Exports to Markdown, Notion, Linear

Drafts the postmortem

You get a document with the timeline filled in, the probable cause flagged, and the blanks that actually need a human — impact, decision, follow-up — left empty.

Shipping in public

We publish every change, including the ones we took back.

The fastest way to judge a young product is to see how it moves. Here is the last fortnight, unedited.

5 Nov 2026 Shipped
Ownership from CODEOWNERS

Nettle now reads CODEOWNERS directly, so paging follows the file you already maintain instead of a second list that drifts.

3 Nov 2026 Fixed
Duplicate pages on flapping checks

A check crossing its threshold repeatedly could page twice in ninety seconds. It now holds for one full interval before escalating.

29 Oct 2026 Shipped
Deploy correlation

If a deploy landed within forty minutes of an incident opening, it is surfaced in the timeline as a likely cause.

24 Oct 2026 Removed
Severity levels 4 and 5

Nobody used them. Two teams told us the picker itself slowed down the first minute of an incident, so there are now three.

21 Oct 2026 Shipped
Phone acknowledgement without the app

Reply to the SMS and the incident is acknowledged. Installing anything at 3am is a bad plan.
