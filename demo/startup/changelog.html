Everything we shipped, and what we took back.

Updated the day it goes out. Removals are listed too — a product that only ever adds is a product nobody is editing.

12 entries
5 Nov 2026Shipped
Ownership from CODEOWNERS

Paging now follows the file you already maintain instead of a second list that drifts out of date.

3 Nov 2026Fixed
Duplicate pages on flapping checks

A check crossing its threshold repeatedly could page twice in ninety seconds. It now holds for one full interval before escalating.

29 Oct 2026Shipped
Deploy correlation

A deploy within forty minutes of an incident opening is surfaced in the timeline as a likely cause, with a one-click rollback link where the provider supports it.

24 Oct 2026Removed
Severity levels 4 and 5

Nobody used them, and two teams said the picker slowed down the first minute of an incident. Three levels now.

21 Oct 2026Shipped
Acknowledge by SMS reply

Reply to the text and the incident is acknowledged. Installing an app at 3am is a bad plan.

17 Oct 2026Fixed
Timeline order across regions

Events from the Sydney checker could land out of order by up to two seconds. Timestamps are now normalised at write.

14 Oct 2026Shipped
Noise report in the weekly digest

Alerts that fired more than three times without a human acting are listed with a one-click mute.

9 Oct 2026Shipped
Microsoft Teams

Incident channels, acknowledgement, and resolution, matching what Slack has had since April.

6 Oct 2026Fixed
Status page uptime rounding

99.985% was displaying as 100%. It now rounds down, always, because rounding uptime up is a lie with good manners.

1 Oct 2026Removed
Weekly summary email to all members

Replaced by one digest to responders only. Six people told us it had become something they filter.

26 Sep 2026Shipped
Public status pages

Generated from real incidents, with a computed uptime figure. Custom domains on every paid plan.

18 Sep 2026Shipped
Drafted postmortems

The timeline, the probable cause, and empty fields for impact, decisions, and follow-up.
