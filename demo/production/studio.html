The building

Unit 4, The Tannery.

A former leather works in the east end, which is as romantic as it sounds until February. We have the top two floors: a 9m by 14m stage with a 4.2m grid, two edit rooms, the grade suite, and a kitchen that is the actual centre of the company.

The stage is not a hire facility and we are not set up to rent it out, but if you are shooting with us it is included and so is the parking, which in this part of the city is worth more than the stage.

Visitors are welcome. There is no reception and the buzzer is the one marked 4 — the one marked Northfall has not worked since 2022.

Working here

We hire about twice a year.

No open roles today. When there are, they go up here and on the usual Scottish crew lists, with the salary in the advert, because a job post without a number wastes everybody's afternoon.

We take two trainees a year through the Screen Scotland scheme, paid, for six months. That one is worth watching for in January.
