Five stages, and you see a cut at three of them.

An hour, usually in person. We leave with what it is for and what success looks like. If we are the wrong studio we say so here, and we will name two who are right.

Free

Week 6+

Terms

The boring part.

Half on signature, half on delivery. For jobs over £50,000, thirds. We invoice on 30 days and we pay our own freelancers on 14, which is the actual reason crews turn up for us in February.

Weather days are budgeted into any shoot that leaves the city, at a reduced rate, and unused ones are credited back. Overtime is at time and a half and we will always ask first.

You own the finished film outright, including the rushes if you want them. Music licensing is quoted separately and in your name so it does not expire with us.
