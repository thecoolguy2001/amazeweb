Getting here

WalkingSix minutes from the market square, down the side of the old post office
BusThe 7 and the 19 stop on Pullman Lane, one minute away

How reserving works

Anything in the shop can be put aside for 48 hours with nothing to pay and no card taken. It is the closest thing we have to a fitting room for a vase.
