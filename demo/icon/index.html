Who makes it

Nearly everything in the shop comes from someone we have met, and quite a lot of it from someone we have had lunch with.

Suffolk · since 2019

Mira Lund

Throws about sixty pieces a month in a converted dairy, all in the same three glazes. We take whatever she has, which is why the carafe is sometimes here and sometimes not.

Lyon · since 1948

Atelier Roux

Three generations of spun brass. The table lamp has been in production, unchanged, since 1961 and the only thing that has altered is the flex.

Yorkshire · since 2012

Dove Press

Letterpress and bookbinding above a bike shop in Hebden Bridge. They will put initials on anything if you give them a fortnight.

Reserving, explained

Anything in the shop can be put aside for 48 hours with nothing to pay. You get a text when it is on the counter with your name on it, and another the morning it is about to go back out.
