The honest bit
This has been sold out online for about a month and there is one on the shelf as of this morning. If you are nearby, switch to reserve and we will put it on the counter; if you are not, the email list for this one is forty people long and the next batch lands in March.
Dimensions and fittings
38cm tall, 22cm shade diameter, 1.9kg. E14 fitting, 40W maximum, bulb not included — we sell the right one for £6 at the counter. UK plug fitted, braided cotton flex in black or oatmeal, tell us which.
Unlacquered brass
It will darken. Within a year it will be noticeably warmer in colour and within five it will be properly aged, unevenly, around where your hand goes. That is the point of unlacquered brass and it cannot be undone, so if you want it bright forever this is the wrong lamp.
Delivery and returns
Packed by hand in the shop, usually out the next working day. Free over £75. Fourteen days to send it back, and we refund the outward postage too if it arrived damaged. Reserved items are never charged until you are standing in front of them.
